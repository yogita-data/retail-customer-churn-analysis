# Retail Customer Churn Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

An exploratory analysis of 100K retail customer records to understand *customer churn patterns, behavioural drivers, and retention opportunities*.

The project focuses on turning customer data into actionable business insights using Python and data visualization.

## 🎯 Business Objective

- Measure overall customer churn
- Identify behavioural patterns associated with churn
- Compare churn across customer segments and channels
- Analyze recency, cart abandonment, loyalty, engagement, and spending
- Identify customers who may need retention attention
- Translate findings into actionable retention strategies

## 📊 Key Findings

- *100,000* customer records analyzed
- Overall churn rate: *46.22%*
- Customers with *271+ days of recency showed more than 97% churn*
- Churn increased from approximately *30% to above 60%* across the lowest to highest cart-abandonment groups
- Churn decreased from approximately *59% to 34%* across the lowest to highest loyalty groups
- Churned customers showed higher recency and cart abandonment, with lower loyalty, engagement, and average spend
- Differences across customer segments were relatively small compared with behavioural differences

  ## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. Build a *recency-triggered retention journey*
2. Target customers showing repeated *cart-abandonment behaviour*
3. Strengthen *engagement and loyalty* initiatives
4. Monitor *high-value customers* showing risky behaviour
5. Measure retention efforts using reactivation rate, repeat purchase rate, incremental revenue, retention cost, and CLV

## 🚦 Retention Priority Framework

Created an analytical prioritization framework based on:

- *45% Recency*
- *30% Cart Abandonment*
- *25% Loyalty*

Customers were grouped into *Low, Medium, High, and Critical* retention-priority levels.

> This is an analytical prioritization framework, not a predictive machine learning model. The relationships observed in the analysis represent associations, not causation.
